## 5. Local storage (arcade / minigames)

The "Arcade" section of this website offers small browser games. When you play one, your highest score is stored locally in your browser (Web Storage / `localStorage`, using keys prefixed `tog:highscore:`). That data stays on your own device: it is never transmitted to us or to any third party, we cannot see it, and no online leaderboard is created from it. Only the score and the time it was achieved are stored — no personal data, no name, no identifier — and no cookies are set for this purpose.

The sole purpose is to show you your own best score again on a later visit. It therefore takes place as part of a function you explicitly requested (Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG / Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). You can delete this data yourself at any time by clearing site data for this website in your browser; the games keep working afterwards, the stored best score simply starts again from zero.
