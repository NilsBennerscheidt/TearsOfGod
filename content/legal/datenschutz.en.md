## 4. Cookies and analytics tools

This website uses no analytics, tracking, or marketing tools. Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights and comparable services are not integrated. No tracking pixels or advertising identifiers are loaded, and there are no third-party embeds (such as YouTube or Spotify players); all videos are self-hosted.

Exactly one technically necessary cookie is set:

**NEXT_LOCALE** — stores your chosen language version of the site (value `de` or `en`). It is a session cookie and is deleted when you close your browser. It contains no identifier, enables no cross-site recognition, and is not transmitted to third parties. It is stored in order to provide the language version you requested and is strictly necessary for that purpose (Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG / Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive); no consent is required for it.

As this website uses no technologies that require consent, no cookie banner is displayed.

## 5. Local storage (arcade / minigames)

The "Arcade" section of this website offers small browser games. When you play one, your highest score is stored locally in your browser (Web Storage / `localStorage`, using keys prefixed `tog:highscore:`). That data stays on your own device: it is never transmitted to us or to any third party, we cannot see it, and no online leaderboard is created from it. Only the score and the time it was achieved are stored — no personal data, no name, no identifier — and no cookies are set for this purpose.

The sole purpose is to show you your own best score again on a later visit. It therefore takes place as part of a function you explicitly requested (Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG / Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). You can delete this data yourself at any time by clearing site data for this website in your browser; the games keep working afterwards, the stored best score simply starts again from zero.
